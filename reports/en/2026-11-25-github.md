## Observations
- Multi-agent control is the hottest single direction. t3code and orca hold the top two spots, [herdrdev/herdr](https://github.com/herdrdev/herdr) grows steadily in the mid-tier, and [getpaseo/paseo](https://github.com/getpaseo/paseo), [jiangmuran/vibepanel](https://github.com/jiangmuran/vibepanel), [jazzenchen/agent-hud-open](https://github.com/jazzenchen/agent-hud-open) and [sun168567/cc-desk-tunnel](https://github.com/sun168567/cc-desk-tunnel) show up among the newcomers or lower rankings. What they share: running several coding agents at once, reachable from a phone or remotely.
- Skills are the default carrier for agent context, and security skills are starting to split off. General frameworks ([obra/superpowers](https://github.com/obra/superpowers), [mattpocock/skills](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills)) have huge installed bases, while today's spikes are vertical skills: security ([Claude-BugHunter](https://github.com/elementalsouls/Claude-BugHunter)), compliance ([app-store-compliance](https://github.com/mjmirza/app-store-compliance)) and design ([diagram-design](https://github.com/cathrynlavery/diagram-design) and others). [NVIDIA/SkillSpector](https://github.com/NVIDIA/SkillSpector), which scans skills before you install them, has grown steadily for 86 days, so the ecosystem is adding an audit step.
- "Reverse, then rebuild" keeps recurring. Visible in the data: [morluto/rea](https://github.com/morluto/rea) (reverse any target), [JuneYaooo/rea-video](https://github.com/JuneYaooo/rea-video) (shot-by-shot video breakdown), erkamyaman/rea-games and rust-anything (rewrite, then check step by step against the original behavior), [Jakeschincariol/replica-skill](https://github.com/Jakeschincariol/replica-skill) (app cloning) and [NiazMorshed2007/reanimate](https://github.com/NiazMorshed2007/reanimate) (frame-by-frame animation reverse engineering). The shared selling point is that the rebuild can be verified.
- Data quality: new_fast_repos contained three one-day-old repos, [GirishSystems1994/reverso-context](https://github.com/GirishSystems1994/reverso-context), [Lukcla3889/postman-agent](https://github.com/Lukcla3889/postman-agent) and [kofimehta06/capture-one-23](https://github.com/kofimehta06/capture-one-23), with 105 to 120 stars each, Windows-download-page copy and topics stuffed with unrelated keywords. All three were dropped.
