- [NVIDIA/auto-ontology](https://github.com/NVIDIA/auto-ontology) · ⭐9,662 · +1,062/day: builds a graph-based semantic reasoning layer over data, for deterministic query generation and better context retrieval. Created in April; ran at 20 to 30 a day until Nov 3.
- [Graphify-Labs/graphify](https://github.com/Graphify-Labs/graphify) · ⭐143,854 · +483/day: turns codebases, docs, SQL schemas and PDFs into a queryable knowledge graph. Deterministic local AST parsing, every edge explained, no vector store. 21 days in a row.
- [yjid/kensaku](https://github.com/yjid/kensaku) · ⭐1,278 · +318/day: local file-search launcher covering docs, code and screenshots (OCR plus semantic search), exposed to agents through MCP.

## Observations

- Skills remain the center of traffic and have split into three groups: official or big-company repos ([cloudflare/security-audit-skill](https://github.com/cloudflare/security-audit-skill), [google/skills](https://github.com/google/skills), anthropics), personal-brand collections ([mattpocock/skills](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills), [pbakaus/impeccable](https://github.com/pbakaus/impeccable), [Nutlope/hallmark](https://github.com/Nutlope/hallmark)), and vertical workflows (security audit, code review, research). A skills security scanner, [NVIDIA/SkillSpector](https://github.com/NVIDIA/SkillSpector), has also grown steadily for 68 days.
- "Reverse-engineer everything" is now a small ecosystem. [morluto/rea](https://github.com/morluto/rea) itself (+3,195/day) is cooling, and the spin-offs are filling in, though at small scale ([JuneYaooo/rea-video](https://github.com/JuneYaooo/rea-video) +834/day, [reacher-z/Awesome-REA](https://github.com/reacher-z/Awesome-REA) +310/day).
- A plugin ecosystem is forming around DeepSeek Harness. The `dsh-plugin` tag appears on [Ebony-Vinyl/dsh-our-free-model](https://github.com/Ebony-Vinyl/dsh-our-free-model), DS-Bot, [dataelement/dsh-desktop](https://github.com/dataelement/dsh-desktop), ds-harness-remote, [poco-ai/Agentero](https://github.com/poco-ai/Agentero), [anywhere-labs/Agents-Anywhere](https://github.com/anywhere-labs/Agents-Anywhere), WeKnora and archify.
