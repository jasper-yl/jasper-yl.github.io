## Observations

- The control layer is consolidating. Daily gains for [stablyai/orca](https://github.com/stablyai/orca), [pingdotgg/t3code](https://github.com/pingdotgg/t3code) and [getpaseo/paseo](https://github.com/getpaseo/paseo) rose together, and [nightly-labs/openbot](https://github.com/nightly-labs/openbot), [herdrdev/herdr](https://github.com/herdrdev/herdr) and [paperclipai/paperclip](https://github.com/paperclipai/paperclip) are growing steadily. Demand has moved from "a better single coding agent" to "managing several existing agents at once and taking over from a phone."
- Token saving is splitting by host. General tools ([JuliusBrussee/caveman](https://github.com/JuliusBrussee/caveman), [rtk-ai/rtk](https://github.com/rtk-ai/rtk), the compression built into [cheaper-inference/OmniRoute](https://github.com/cheaper-inference/OmniRoute)) are still rising, and two Antigravity-specific plugins ([INK-Q/agy-fast](https://github.com/INK-Q/agy-fast), [zou172866/Antigravity-agy-dart](https://github.com/zou172866/Antigravity-agy-dart)) have grown for 5 to 7 days. That suggests an Antigravity plugin ecosystem is starting, but two projects are all the data shows, so it's not an established trend.
- Skills are top-heavy. [obra/superpowers](https://github.com/obra/superpowers), [mattpocock/skills](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills) and [anthropics/skills](https://github.com/anthropics/skills) all have 200K+ stars and gain 500 to 850/day. Newcomers mostly target vertical niches like design and video ([cathrynlavery/diagram-design](https://github.com/cathrynlavery/diagram-design), [felipebasurto/unslop-ui](https://github.com/felipebasurto/unslop-ui), [petergyang/video-explainer](https://github.com/petergyang/video-explainer), [Moh4696/inkworks-skill](https://github.com/Moh4696/inkworks-skill)). As skills multiply, a pre-install scanner like [NVIDIA/SkillSpector](https://github.com/NVIDIA/SkillSpector) has also found steady growth.
- Data noise: three repos created 11-24 (kofimehta06/capture-one-23, Lukcla3889/postman-agent, GirishSystems1994/reverso-context) show 216 to 222 stars and +96 to +117/day. Their descriptions are "Windows 10/11 version" download pages with unrelated keywords stuffed into topics. They have nothing to do with agents and aren't organic growth, so they were removed. Also removed as off-topic: the same-named qianyiai/kurocat and chanke17/KuroCat (desktop pets), and minimind, crossdesk and phonebox, which have little to do with agents.
