## 4. Observations

- **Security Skills have the strongest momentum.** `security-audit-skill` has been second in growth for two days running, and `Claude-Red` and `SkillSpector` are also on the list, so both the generation side (auditing, offense and defense) and the protection side (scanning Skills) are growing.
- **Design and video Skills are emerging in batches.** `hallmark` +1477, `lemo-opuscar` +645, `hyperframes` +677, `openfilm` +263, and these also make up the majority of today's new-repo list. By comparison, compression and memory projects are growing steadily, with no new breakouts.
- **Interest in the retrieval layer is cooling.** `xerj` fell from +1180 to +255, and `graphify` is roughly flat. I only have these two days to compare, so I can't yet say whether this is a trend, and it needs a few more days of observation.
- **The dsh ecosystem still dominates traffic.** `deepseek-harness` is at +979/day and `dsh-our-free-model` at +1767/day (yesterday +2120). The latter draws attention with its "free and unlimited" pitch, a claim that has not been verified and does not reflect progress in context capabilities themselves.
