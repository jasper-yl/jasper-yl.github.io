## Observations

1. Computer use is expanding from the browser to desktop and phone. agent-desktop (desktop, accessibility tree), Mika (Android), [trycua/cua](https://github.com/trycua/cua) (sandboxes, steady growth) and [reacher-z/Awesome-CUA](https://github.com/reacher-z/Awesome-CUA) (directory) all show up together. agent-desktop got 1 to 4 stars a day for 10 days and now gains +3,408/day.
2. The DeepSeek Harness ecosystem has taken shape. The main repo has ⭐270,844 and a 22-day streak, and dsh-our-free-model has 30 days. The rising list adds [JialaoLiu/dsh-baton](https://github.com/JialaoLiu/dsh-baton), [SAOS-3624/dsh-video-storyboard](https://github.com/SAOS-3624/dsh-video-storyboard), Deepseek-Harness-Local-Android (⭐460, +13) and ds-harness-remote (+135 on Trending), and cc-switch added a deepseek-harness topic. Plugins (model providers, desktop, remote, mobile) account for most of the growth.
3. Skills are moving from methodology toward taste, design and domain-specific packs. impeccable, hallmark, taste-skill and ui-ux-pro-max are all on the list. Cloudflare, Google, NVIDIA and Microsoft are also shipping skill and cost-control projects (security-audit-skill, elephant-goldfish-model, SkillSpector, TokenEconomizer).
4. New data-ontology and knowledge-graph projects are appearing beyond code graphs: auto-ontology (NVIDIA) and helix-foundry (HelixDB, ⭐2,011, created 10-07), which uses agents to draft ontologies and load them into a graph database. That points to the context layer extending to data, not just code. Both have only a day or two of data, so no conclusion yet.
