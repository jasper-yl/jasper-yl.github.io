## 4. Observations

- **Security is the strongest category among Skills.** `cloudflare/security-audit-skill` (+2177) and `Claude-Red` (+641) rank near the top of Skill growth. `NVIDIA/SkillSpector` (+142) is a reminder that Skills themselves should be scanned before installing.
- **Agent isolation and runtimes have gone from single tools to a group of products.** `OpenShell`, `sandbox-runtime`, `orca` and `herdr` all made the list at once. Sandboxes take the OS-level, lightweight, no-container route, while the management layer is about running many agents in parallel.
- **"Getting coding Agents to produce non-code output" is a concentrated theme among new repos.** Video (`lemo-opuscar`, `openfilm`, `vvv`), audio (`earshot`), CAD (`solidcraft`) and real devices (`esp32-iphone-agent`) are all on today's new-repo chart, with the common thread of plugging into existing Agents as Skills or MCP.
- **The dsh ecosystem continues to dominate traffic.** `deepseek-harness` has 260,244 stars, `rea`, `archify`, `ruflo` and others all carry `dsh-plugin`-related tags, and the top new repo, `dsh-our-free-model`, grew +2120/day. However, much of that growth comes from the appeal of "free models" and does not necessarily indicate progress in context capabilities themselves.
