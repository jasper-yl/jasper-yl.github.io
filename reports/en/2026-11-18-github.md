## Observations

- **Domain-knowledge packs are still the growth engine, but they follow a spike-then-fade shape.** Claude-BugHunter, rust-anything and modern-web-guidance each went from single or double digits to thousands within a day or two. modern-web-guidance has already dropped from 4,137 on 11-14 to 1,169 (a similar call was made in the 11-17 report). Whether they hold depends on whether each still gains a few hundred a day.
- **The skill ecosystem is growing a support layer.** NVIDIA's SkillEvaluator (evaluation) and SkillSpector (security scanning, 79-day streak), Cloudflare's security-audit-skill, and Google's elephant-goldfish-model (context engineering) suggest competition is moving from writing skills to evaluating and auditing them.
- **The multi-agent orchestration and console category is splitting into tiers.** Established projects (t3code, orca, herdr, paseo) have been steady for dozens to over a hundred days, while newcomers (LoopForge, SoduWork) are ramping up. SoduWork's daily growth rate is 68.1%, but it has only ⭐2,716, so it needs a few more days to show whether it lasts.
- **Data quality:** over ten repos named `ai--manus2.0` and `GitHub-Copilot-app` share the same name and description (created 11-14, 369 to 384 stars, 0 new stars yesterday). A batch of `blender-ai-copilot-*` and `Blender-AI-MCP-Studio` repos (created 11-12, 510 to 516 stars, 0 to 2 new stars yesterday) looks similar. Both batches were dropped and are not counted in any list.
