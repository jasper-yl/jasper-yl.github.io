## 4. Observations

- The REA wave is cooling, but its derivative ecosystem is growing. rea fell from 17,573 to 5,147 per day and still beats every other project. Downstream, there are resource lists (Awesome-REA, awesome-reverse-engineering), a defensive tool (bla), and reverse-engineering skills for games: [Jakeschincariol/replica-skill](https://github.com/Jakeschincariol/replica-skill) (⭐6,594), [rehan-remade/universal-modder](https://github.com/rehan-remade/universal-modder) (⭐9,226) and [cranebeaker/coop-anything](https://github.com/cranebeaker/coop-anything) (⭐1,618). Basis: the topics or descriptions of these projects mention reverse engineering directly.
- dsh (DeepSeek Harness) is becoming a plugin platform. deepseek-harness has 266,563 ⭐ and +949/day, with its 7-day average at or above 666 for 19 days. Projects with the `dsh-plugin` topic cover model access (dsh-our-free-model), a desktop app (dsh-desktop, ⭐17,031), multi-bot teams ([Fei2411/DS-Bot](https://github.com/Fei2411/DS-Bot), ⭐4,619, +124/day), MCP handoff (dsh-baton), a chart skill (archify) and a knowledge base (WeKnora). [farion1231/cc-switch](https://github.com/farion1231/cc-switch) (⭐150,358, +332/day) also lists DeepSeek Harness as a supported target. Growth is concentrating on this host, but each project is growing for its own reasons, so don't read them as one trend.
- "Have a coding agent make video" has become its own category. Rising together: hyperframes (⭐75,864, +603/day, 54 days running), video-shotcraft (+485/day), lemo-opuscar (+344/day), OpenMontage (+310/day), openfilm (⭐3,721, +184/day), kinetograph and vvv. Skills are expanding from writing code to making video and design; the anti-slop design skills (impeccable, hallmark, taste-skill) fit the same pattern.
- One-task repos are piling up. Besides cleanyourtv (⭐46,292), similar projects [simbasjungles/free-my-tv](https://github.com/simbasjungles/free-my-tv) (⭐5,450, +111/day), [Ansub/tv-detox](https://github.com/Ansub/tv-detox) (⭐1,847, +115/day) and cleanyourtv-app (⭐1,156, +74/day) were all created in mid-to-late October. Projects that use a coding agent to do one small, specific job grow fast, but cleanyourtv has already dropped from its Oct 20 peak of +6,809 to +1,351, so its momentum looks limited.
- No repo could be clearly identified as star-farming, so none were removed. Noise removed: pure model relays such as freellmapi and sub2api, and NSFW or fitness repos with little connection to agents.
