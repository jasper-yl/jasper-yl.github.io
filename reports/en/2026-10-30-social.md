**What kinds of features go viral (judgment)**
1. "Free" plus "no key / no login": dsh at +1,863/day, 28 tutorial posts, almost no influencer involvement.
2. One prompt that makes an agent do something surprising, with a dramatic demo: REA's DJI drone (❤3,224) and "recover deleted photos" (❤636).
3. A visible before-and-after or a memorable metaphor: cleanyourtv's TV home screen, impeccable's de-AI-ing, EGM's "elephant and goldfish".
4. Backing from a big company or well-known institution with a clear mechanism: Cloudflare's "the bug finder can't confirm its own finding", Google's EGM. But when amplification comes mainly from list posts, as with security-audit-skill, organic discussion stays thin.
