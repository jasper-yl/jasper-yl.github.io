## Observations
- Multi-agent consoles are the main category. orca, t3code, openbot, SoduWork, paseo, tarobird and agent-hud-open all share one shape: a single interface that manages several coding agents at once, usually with a phone client. [vercel/board](https://github.com/vercel/board) in the rising stars suggests a major vendor is entering too.
- Skills are specializing, and similar projects are crowding together. Design de-slopping has impeccable, hallmark, taste-skill and unslop-anything; reverse-and-reimplement skills have the rea family.
- Token and cache optimization is splitting by platform. The general tools are caveman and rtk; today's newcomers are Antigravity-specific (agy-fast, agy-dart). Their savings figures (-34%, -31%, -40%) are the repos' own claims, and nothing in the data verifies them independently.
- The dsh (DeepSeek Harness) plugin ecosystem is spreading. The official repo has ⭐287,985; dsh-desktop is in the steady-growth list; dsh-windows-mcp, moa-plus, Mebular and DSH-projects are among the rising stars; and opengame, archify and last30days on the main list carry the `dsh-plugin` topic.

  - 3 new repos with names imitating well-known desktop software (351–357 stars, +133–139/day) and generic software-download copy; not agent projects.
  - Two repos from the everyinfra org, seo-skills and everyinfra-agent-plugins, with nearly identical daily gains (e.g. 47/43, 43/46, 34/34); left out of the rising stars.
  - Several video-generation, desktop-pet, database-client and model-API-relay projects, dropped as noise.
