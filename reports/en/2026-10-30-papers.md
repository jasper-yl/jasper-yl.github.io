## 一、🔥 Hot list: Agent Context picks

### [Δτ-Bench: Pushing Agents Toward a Moving Capability](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.34238)
- Heat: HF 👍28 (3 days on the list, +8); code: [zjunlp/delta-tau-bench](https://github.com/zjunlp/delta-tau-bench), 5 stars.
- Problem: Tool ecosystems change. Tools disappear, get replaced, or gain more efficient alternatives, so learned skills and procedural knowledge go stale.
- Method: The benchmark has 1,588 verified multi-turn tasks across 42 domains and 6 successive ecosystem states. The authors also propose Steward, an ecosystem manager that maintains skills incrementally during execution and supplies tools dynamically.
- Result: The strongest model or harness reaches only 42.3% task success. Early gains from existing skill-learning methods don't necessarily last as the ecosystem shifts. Steward keeps higher success under change, with lower skill-maintenance overhead.

### [Evolving to Match Capabilities: Improving Harness Self-Evolution via Skill Adaptation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.33024)
- Heat: HF 👍32 (3 days on the list, +21); code: [zhihaoyang30/skill-adaptation-code](https://github.com/zhihaoyang30/skill-adaptation-code), 6 stars.
- Problem: The same skill can help a strong model and hurt a weak one.
- Method: Across 7 models and 3 benchmarks, the authors found 103 task-skill pairs. In one, a skill lifted GPT-5.6-luna by 31.1 points and dropped Gemma-4-E4B by 8.8. In weak-model failures, 86% of the violations were already spelled out in the skill. The authors sort failures into four types (tool execution, context management, instruction following, verification) and distill them into a fixed meta-skill. A self-evolution loop then uses it to rewrite skills to fit the executor's ability.
- Result: With Kimi-K3 as an external rewriter, 3 weak executors gained 5.1 to 14.1 points over the original skill. After five rounds of self-evolution, the meta-skill added another 2.0 to 5.1 points to the final scores of 7 executors.

### [DLite: History-free Speculative Decoding for Agentic AI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.23787)
- Heat: HF 👍36 (7 days on the list, +3); code: [SJTU-DAI-Sys/DLite](https://github.com/SJTU-DAI-Sys/DLite), 49 stars.
- Problem: With the long contexts and multi-turn interaction of agents, existing draft models lose up to 80% of their accepted length, and storing history KV cache adds system overhead.
- Method: DLite keeps no history context. It reuses the target model's contextual hidden states for local speculative prediction, restores within-block causal dependencies with a lightweight sequential head, and uses two-stage training.
- Result: Accepted length reaches 2 to 6, up to 70% higher than the state-of-the-art DSpark.

### [Build, Don't Play: Self-Improving LLM Research Agents Develop a Hearthstone Battlegrounds AI (AutoTavern)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.33845)
- Heat: HF 👍26 (3 days on the list, +18); code: [MCG-NJU/AutoTavern](https://github.com/MCG-NJU/AutoTavern), 165 stars.
- Problem: Can an LLM agent take a complex real-world problem from raw data to a system that competes with top human players?
- Method: Several specialized research agents share one typed research memory. Every conclusion must pass a verification gate, and results from live games feed back in. The agents use this to improve the simulator, opponents and strategy, and their own research process. Humans set only the goal and rules.
- Result: In 20 days it built TavernGym and 23 versions of TavernAgent. Ladder rating rose from 6,000 to about 15,000. Over the last 20 games it averaged a 1.60 placement with an 80% first-place rate, against 2.34 and 59% for the server's top-10 players.

### [DeltaStreamer: Towards Human-Like Agentic Streaming Video Understanding](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.19849)
- Heat: HF 👍38 (7 days on the list, +0); code: [BBBBchan/DeltaStreamer](https://github.com/BBBBchan/DeltaStreamer), 87 stars.
- Problem: Streaming video has to keep evidence without knowing future questions. Compressing history blurs event transitions, loses detail, and can't be recovered.
- Method: Before a question arrives, the model watches the stream continuously and writes visual changes explicitly into a structured change-aware memory. At question time, a query-driven planner decides what evidence is needed, then guides memory retrieval and targeted replay of past frames.
- Result: It scores 66.07% on OVO-Bench and 59.53% on StreamingBench, ahead of representative streaming methods.

## 二、🔥 Hot list: other Agent directions

DataWorld-35B-A3B, trained with RL from zero, beats its base by 20.90 points on DABstep-Hard. Code: [Zhanli-Li/Data-World](https://github.com/Zhanli-Li/Data-World), 27 stars.
- [What Effort Level Should I Choose? On the Economics of Reasoning Effort in LLM Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.34491) · HF 👍32 (+24) · 2 days: high reasoning effort often fails on tasks that low effort can solve. On easy tasks every level solves, top effort costs up to 42 times as much. Starting at the lowest effort and escalating after failed verification matches or beats the alternatives at lower cost. Code: [KaiserWhoLearns/ReasoningEffortLLM](https://github.com/KaiserWhoLearns/ReasoningEffortLLM), 66 stars.
- [RoboHarness: A Simple Harness Outperforms VLA and World Action Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.14018) · HF 👍22 (+20) · 3 days: gives a coding agent a visual-geometric control panel, and Qwen3.8-Flash-Next beats the state-of-the-art VLA on BEHAVIOR Challenge 2025 tasks. - [SCOPE: Compiling Natural-Language Policies into Neurosymbolic Predicate Programs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31062) · HF 👍31 (+25) · 7 days: compiles natural-language policies into predicate programs built from small models, deterministic extractors and code, for agent policy enforcement. Programs optimized on the weakest executor transfer to other models, with steadier predictions and explainable verdicts.
- [EdgeWise: Bringing Long-Horizon Agents to the Edge via Selective Cloud Delegation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.17560) · HF 👍30 · 7 days: a small local model is the main solver and hands only critical steps to a cloud model. Step-level RL jointly optimizes solving and delegation. Inference cost drops by up to 62% on SWE-Rebench and 34% on Terminal-Bench 2.1.
- [Agent Mosaic: Recognizing Confidentiality Leaks in Agentic LLM Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30418) · HF 👍26 (+10) · 6 days: agents piece together sensitive conclusions from documents they can legitimately access (the "mosaic" effect). Even the best model, GPT-5.6-Sol, leaks a forbidden combined conclusion in 7.08% of answers to benign queries. With classification headers and aggregation rules added, 32.37% of answers in simulation were still unsafe. Code: [ZQ-Dev8/agent-mosaic](https://github.com/ZQ-Dev8/agent-mosaic), 1 star.

## 四、🆕 New: published last night
- [Don't Lose the Thread: Empowering Long-Horizon LLM Agents with Cognitive Resource Self-Allocation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40350) (CORAL): a working-memory tool the agent can call. It archives progress and verified facts, then resets the cluttered context and starts a new round. An 8B model scores 42.8% on GAIA with 6 to 7 times fewer input tokens than ReAct.
- [MemSteer: Turning User Histories into Supervision for Agent Memory Optimization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.39276): turns unlabeled user histories into evidence-backed QA supervision for optimizing memory storage, retrieval and context construction. ATM-Bench Question Score is 13.4 points above Base-BM25.
- [MUSE: Multi-Level Memory with Selective Runtime Injection for Scientific Coding Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.38332): builds three memory levels (global, method, implementation) from past trajectories, and an LLM gate decides at run time whether to inject experience or skip it. It beats retrieval and memory baselines on all 4 benchmarks. Code: [hr-jin/MUSE](https://github.com/hr-jin/MUSE).
- [Context Compaction for World Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.38980): applies agent-harness context compaction to world models. Without retraining, it retrieves rooms from 4 times beyond the context window.
- [FlatCake: A Flat KVCache Storage Architecture for LLM Serving](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.38355): a KV cache storage design that treats DRAM and NVMe as peer tiers. On real Claude Code traces (DeepSeek-V4-Flash), cache hit rate holds at 94% (Mooncake: 64%), and average TTFT at peak load drops 1.6 times.
- [HEDGE: Gated Hypergraph Memory for Scalable Multi-Agent Debate](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.38902): stores debate trajectories in a semantic hypergraph, and a learned selection module decides what to store, back up or discard. Average accuracy rises 5.0 points, memory use falls 48%, and debate rounds fall 34%.
- [Compile Away Uncertainty: Self-Evolving Agent Skills via Programmatic Compilation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.39508) (SkillCompiler): compiles the explicit operations in a skill into verified programs, leaves context-dependent judgment to the LLM, and reuses the program assets. Task performance improves by up to 32.33%.
- [Before the Next Rollout: Diagnosing Skill Utility Shifts from Policy Updates](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.39358): a policy update changes the marginal utility of the same skill. Diagnosing with policy-update signals guides skill evolution and cuts token use.
- [When Skills Tell Agents Not to Ask: A Large-Scale Study of Oversight Directives in Agent Skills](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37734): analyzes 1.53 million skills. About 1 in 17 (roughly 88,000) contains instructions that loosen oversight, such as `--no-verify`. Open-source scanners catch at most 46%.
- [Selective and Hybrid-State Communication for Latent Multi-Agent Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37779): agents communicate through KV state. SelectKV cuts transmitted KV positions by about 9.7 to 9.9%. HyLaMAS sends KV and recurrent state together and cuts decoded tokens by 53 to 80%.
- [Finding Is Not Returning, Returning Is Not Reconstructing: A Multi-Dimensional Evaluation of Agent Social Re-entry](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.39781): splits an agent's return to an old conversation into finding it, going back, and rebuilding context. Persistent memory raised the proactive reply rate but gave no consistent gain in context reconstruction.
- [Beyond LLM Wrappers: Context Engineering and the Three-Engine Architecture for Reliable Financial AI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.39230): an architecture of a knowledge graph, a deterministic optimization engine and a multi-agent LLM layer, where "context engineering" selects, ranks, assembles and verifies context. From about 11 months of observing 15 deployments, hallucination fell from roughly 7 to 9% to under 1% (the authors stress this is observational, not causal).

## 五、Research trends
- Skill and harness maintenance has moved from "how to write a skill" to "how to keep it adapted and current." Δτ-Bench (ecosystem drift), Skill Adaptation (differences in model capability), and two New papers, SkillCompiler and Skill utility shifts, each attack it from a different angle.
- Long-horizon context management has a training-side and an inference-side approach. On the training side, Mosaic (rising) treats compaction and forking as multi-session RL. On the inference side, CORAL (New) has the agent archive and reset on its own. FlatCake and DLite work from the serving side, on KV cache and speculative decoding for long agentic contexts.
- The GitHub leaders are Intern-Decision (775 stars), OpenGCUA (441), Self-Evolving Agent Harness (387), RoboHarness (279) and AutoTavern (165). Their themes (harnesses, division of decision-making, computer use) roughly match where paper heat is concentrated.
- Skill security is starting to get systematic study: the 1.53-million-skill directive scan (New) and Agent Mosaic's information leaks (hot). These are independent papers, not one shared risk.
