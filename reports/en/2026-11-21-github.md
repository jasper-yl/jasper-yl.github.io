- Orchestration and consoles are the steadiest line: [pingdotgg/t3code](https://github.com/pingdotgg/t3code) (34 days), [stablyai/orca](https://github.com/stablyai/orca) (139 days), [herdrdev/herdr](https://github.com/herdrdev/herdr) (154 days in the steady group) and [getpaseo/paseo](https://github.com/getpaseo/paseo), plus [mdiri-ai/SoduWork](https://github.com/mdiri-ai/SoduWork), [Tencent/LoopForge](https://github.com/Tencent/LoopForge) and [backnotprop/plannotator](https://github.com/backnotprop/plannotator) still on today's list. - Burst-style vertical skill packs still dominate the daily-growth list ([Claude-BugHunter](https://github.com/elementalsouls/Claude-BugHunter), [rust-anything](https://github.com/erkamyaman/rust-anything), [app-store-compliance](https://github.com/mjmirza/app-store-compliance), [ffmpeg-skill](https://github.com/kajisho5/ffmpeg-skill)), but most fall back within 3 to 4 days of peaking. BugHunter is the counterexample and rose again today. Skills collections and design skills are the long-term stable ones.
- Two newer patterns are forming. One is agents that control phones ([phonedriver](https://github.com/hridaya423/phonedriver), [pinky](https://github.com/DIYgod/pinky), poki, phone-harness). The other is official vendor skills (Chrome, Cloudflare, Google Stitch, Cursor), where the leading projects tend to grow fast first and slow after the vendor arrives. The two Chrome repos are an example.
- The plugin ecosystem around DeepSeek Harness (dsh) is expanding. Projects with the dsh-plugin or deepseek-harness topic on today's list include [dsh-our-free-model](https://github.com/Ebony-Vinyl/dsh-our-free-model), dsh-desktop, [dsh-windows-mcp](https://github.com/helloHub1212/dsh-windows-mcp), openmotion, archify, huashu-design, [ai4ai](https://github.com/AI4XE/ai4ai) and agent-purge.
