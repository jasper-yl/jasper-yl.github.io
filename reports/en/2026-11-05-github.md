## 4. Observations

- REA has spawned a small supply chain. The original has 433,458 ⭐ and +3,573/day. Around it: the video version rea-video, the animation version [NiazMorshed2007/reanimate](https://github.com/NiazMorshed2007/reanimate) (frame-by-frame animation reverse engineering), the directory Awesome-REA (⭐1,000, +58/day, 7 days running), and the counter-project bla. "Reverse-engineer anything" is turning from a single project into a skill category.
- Skills are moving from general engineering methodology (superpowers, mattpocock) toward vertical creative work. Design anti-slop (impeccable, hallmark, taste-skill) and "video / animation from code" (hyperframes, OpenMontage, lemo-opuscar, cinecode, code-animation-prompts) are charting together, and this kind of project fills a large share of the rising list too.
- DeepSeek Harness (dsh) now has its own plugin ecosystem. The board has the core deepseek-harness (⭐273,607, 24 days running), the dsh-desktop app (mid-size list, 81 days running), ds-harness-remote for remote access, and the DS-Bot multi-bot plugin (⭐5,704, +166/day). On the Context side, graph and ontology tools (NVIDIA auto-ontology, graphify, codegraph) and long-term memory (claude-mem speeding up) are the two steadiest lines in today's data.
