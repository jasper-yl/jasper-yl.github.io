- What makes a feature spread fastest:
  - Big-company or official backing plus a skill or CLI you can install immediately spreads fastest (Cloudflare, Vercel, NVIDIA, the Perplexity integration).
  - A specific pain everyone complains about is easier for KOLs to repeat: AI-slop interfaces, right-database-wrong-answer queries, false positives in LLM security scans.
