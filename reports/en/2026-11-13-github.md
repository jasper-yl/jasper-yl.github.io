- Skills are the biggest category, and design taste is the steadiest subgroup. taste-skill, impeccable, hallmark (⭐54,381, +525/day), ui-ux-pro-max-skill (+444/day) and anti-slop all sit at several hundred stars a day. - Platform owners are publishing directly for agents. Today's entries include Google Chrome's modern-web-guidance, vercel-labs/ui, meta/astryx, expo/exact, openai/codex-security, and NVIDIA's SkillEvaluator and SkillSpector. Agents as consumers are now shaping how infrastructure projects are designed.
- Two lines run in parallel: "give the agent its own computer or phone" and "teams of bots." Device-operating projects are andee, DIYgod/pinky (⭐2,072), poki and agent-desktop. Bot-team and Grok Bot alternatives are CopilotKit/OpenBot, OpenMausBot (⭐22,084, +475/day), kwant and agentes-chat.
