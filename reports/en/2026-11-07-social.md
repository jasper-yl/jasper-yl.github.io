- **What spreads fastest**:
  1. A capability that fits in one sentence and is easy to reproduce, such as REA (take apart an app without source code) or cleanyourtv (give Claude a TV model and it cleans the TV). These generate the most videos.
  2. A big-company brand open-sourcing an internal method (Cloudflare, Google, NVIDIA). An official launch plus influencer relays is enough to get traction, and there is little pushback.
  3. A skill aimed at a specific pain point, like AI-slop design: one-line install, visible results, and videos follow naturally. Today's gains are small, though.
