- **What makes a feature spread fast**:
  1. A big-company brand plus a skill or plugin that drops straight into existing agents. Cloudflare, Google, Anthropic, Meta and DeepSeek are all on the list, and a one-command install into Claude Code or Codex is the lowest barrier.
  2. A strong hook such as "reverse everything" or "No. 1 repo" gets amplified by listicle and tutorial accounts (rea and its offshoot rea-video), but real usage feedback is far smaller than the exposure.
  3. Methodology-style skills that address homogeneous agent output or coding before thinking (split review, anti-AI-slop) tend to draw more organic discussion. Evidence is thin today, so this is a trend, not a conclusion.
