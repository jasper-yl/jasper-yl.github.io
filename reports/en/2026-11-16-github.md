## Observations

- Official agent content from platform vendors is the strongest new source of growth in the data, and it fades fast.
  - modern-web-guidance peaked at 4,142 (11-14) and fell to 2,592. astryx peaked at 3,157 (11-09) and is at 815 today. vercel-labs/ui peaked at 2,412 (11-12) and is at 404. chrome-devtools-mcp jumped on 11-13 and has held at about 780.
  - Participants include Google, Meta, Vercel, NVIDIA, Tencent, n8n and Cloudflare.
  - The data doesn't show how any of this growth spread.
- The REA (reverse-engineering) cluster is cooling: rea's 7-day average is 2,647 against 4,937 over 28 days.
  - Spin-offs are still growing: rea-video +553 and [reacher-z/Awesome-REA](https://github.com/reacher-z/Awesome-REA) +90 (⭐2,982).
  - [qybaihe/bla](https://github.com/qybaihe/bla) (⭐5,891, +120/day) protects code from analysis by AI reverse-engineering agents. It is on the list too, as the defensive side of the same topic.
- Growth is moving from "more agents and skills" to "keeping agents under control": evaluation (SkillEvaluator +977), review (open-code-review +951, plannotator +679), human-approval orchestration (LoopForge +972), and skill security scanning (SkillSpector, 77 straight days).
  - Memory layers (claude-mem, hindsight) have come down from their highs to steady growth.
  - Cost control is picking up: TokenEconomizer is at +784/day, against +101 to +172 in the 11-10 to 11-12 reports.
- Two batches of repos were removed from the lists as data-quality problems:
  - About 10 Blender AI copilot / MCP repos, all created 11-12 with near-identical descriptions, from different accounts.
    - About 8 of them have ⭐497-514 and +48 to +55/day. The rest differ more in star count.
    - The largest, [hitkeeen/blender-ai-mcp-addon](https://github.com/hitkeeen/blender-ai-mcp-addon), gained +201 the day before and +1,444 yesterday.
    - Their descriptions are keyword-stuffed, and all were removed.
  - About 15 same-name clones of "GitHub-Copilot-app" / "ai--manus2.0", all created 11-14.
    - They have about ⭐370-384, and the data shows delta_1d of -277 to -288 within a day, meaning stars dropped. Removed.
  - [supercli-site/xiaobai-kanban](https://github.com/supercli-site/xiaobai-kanban) (⭐534) gained 49-51 a day for 7 straight days, and [Cattmonkey/ChaosIntelligence](https://github.com/Cattmonkey/ChaosIntelligence) (⭐464) has gained a constant 18 a day for a long stretch.
