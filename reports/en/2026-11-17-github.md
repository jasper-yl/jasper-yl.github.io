## Observations
- **Knowledge and guidance packs drove today's growth.**
  - Chrome's official modern-web-guidance went from 15 a day to 4,139 a day in about a week. Claude-BugHunter, claude-trading-skills and stitch-skills also jumped within the last 5 days.
  - All of them package domain knowledge from an authoritative source or a vertical expert as context an agent can call directly.
  - Peaks may fade quickly: modern-web-guidance was at 2,631 yesterday, down from 4,139 on 11-14.
- **Multi-agent orchestration and "unified console" tools are steady and crowded.**
  - t3code, orca, LoopForge, SoduWork, paseo, herdr, nightly-labs/openbot and facadecode all drive Claude Code, Codex and others at once.
  - Older projects rely on long, stable growth (orca, 135 days). New ones take off on big-company backing (Tencent, n8n).
  - The dsh (DeepSeek Harness) ecosystem is also expanding: deepseek-harness has ⭐282,869, dsh-desktop has climbed steadily for 93 days, dsh-our-free-model has ⭐78,398 at +1,256/day, and dsh-plugin shows up in many repo topics.
- **Evaluation and security are catching up after the skill explosion.**
  - NVIDIA has both SkillEvaluator (+844/day) and SkillSpector (78 days running), and Cloudflare's security-audit-skill is also on the list.
  - MCP servers and CLIs that give agents access to real environments (browsers, phones, web pages) keep arriving: browse, poki, pinky, webrouter, chrome-devtools-mcp.
- **Many same-name clones appeared on the list and were removed.**
  - The new-repo list has a batch of identically named `ai--manus2.0` and `GitHub-Copilot-app` repos, created 11-14 with identical descriptions, 369–380 stars each and a delta_1d of 0.
  - Another batch of `blender-ai-copilot-*` / `Blender-AI-MCP-*` repos, created 11-12, has near-identical descriptions, 492–516 stars and a delta_1d of only 0–3.
  - Near-identical star counts with vague or templated descriptions point to star farming or bulk cloning, so none are listed.
  - xiaobai-kanban (about +49 to +52 for 7 days straight) and ChaosIntelligence (exactly +18 every day for 7 days) grow too evenly and were also left out.
