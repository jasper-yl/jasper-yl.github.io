## 4. Observations

- Reverse engineering has become a small ecosystem. [morluto/rea](https://github.com/morluto/rea) has 395,650 stars and a 23-day streak. Three new lists (Awesome-REA, awesome-reverse-engineered, awesome-reverse-engineer-anything) are on multi-day runs, and [morluto/awesome-reverse-engineering](https://github.com/morluto/awesome-reverse-engineering) (⭐1,307, +38/day) is also rising. [rehan-remade/universal-modder](https://github.com/rehan-remade/universal-modder), which has Claude Code mod games, is 27 days running. The `rea` topic tag shows up across these projects.
- The dsh ecosystem is taking shape. The host, deepseek-harness, holds at 700–1,500 a day, surrounded by projects tagged `dsh-plugin`: dsh-our-free-model, DS-Bot, archify, ruflo and others. So far the "plugins" are mostly model access and multi-agent setups.
- Skills and their security tooling are appearing together. Cloudflare's security audit skill and NVIDIA's SkillSpector are both climbing, which suggests people are starting to care about auditing skills themselves. The multi-agent control panels (orca, herdr, thinkrail, openbot, squadra) suggest the pain point has moved from "is a single agent any good" to "managing several at once".
- A separate hot category is non-agent "one prompt, hand it to Claude Code" projects. The TV ad-removal family spreads on that pattern: [Gathin23/cleanyourtv](https://github.com/Gathin23/cleanyourtv) (⭐40,994, +3,651/day, 10 days running), [simbasjungles/free-my-tv](https://github.com/simbasjungles/free-my-tv) (⭐4,946) and [Ansub/tv-detox](https://github.com/Ansub/tv-detox) (⭐1,664). These are use cases, not agent technology progress, so they are not on the chart.
