## Observations

- **REA and "agent-ready" are both on the board.** The REA cluster is still there but cooling: the 7-day average of 3,327 is under half the 28-day average of 8,378. Meanwhile projects from Meta, Google, Expo, Vercel, Cloudflare and NVIDIA are all on the list (astryx, modern-web-guidance, exact, agent-browser, agent-desktop, security-audit-skill). What they share is that coding agents can use them directly.
- **Skills are splitting.** The official repo peaked and fell (anthropics/skills from 1,535 to 958), while individual and big-company engineering-method repos are still accelerating (addyosmani/agent-skills 317 → 765 over four days). The design-focused "anti-AI-slop" skills (hallmark +821, impeccable +712, taste-skill +493) hold at a few hundred a day.
- **Context work splits into two ends: cheaper compression and structured retrieval.** billion-context (+656), caveman (+239) and TokenEconomizer (+101) address cost. graphify (+547) and auto-ontology (+381, past peak) address graphs and semantic layers. Among memory projects, claude-mem has settled at about 400–460/day and hindsight holds around 200–300.
- **Several old or obscure repos jumped with no visible source of traffic, so treat them as reference only.** They include modern-web-guidance, ha-mcp, gbrain, daily_stock_analysis and claude-quickstarts (it ran 5–28/day before; the last 3 days were 470 → 555 → 481, then +382 today, falling). There is also a cluster about having agents make video and motion graphics in code: rea-video (+802), hyperframes (⭐82,697, +564/day, 46 straight days), lemo-opuscar (+402) and pixelfx (+327).
