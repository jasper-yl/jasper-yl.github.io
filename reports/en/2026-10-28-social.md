- What kinds of features spread fastest:
  1. A "dangerous / can do anything" capability that fits in one sentence, plus one install command (REA's "reverse-engineer any app", cleanyourtv's "strip the ads off your TV"). Big accounts and YouTube amplify it, but real usage feedback doesn't keep up.
  2. "Free + no key + four-step tutorial" (dsh-our-free-model, magpie with free models) gets many individual accounts to post in imitation, and also brings permission and terms-of-use disputes.
  3. Big-company backing plus a concrete metaphor (Google's elephant and goldfish, Cloudflare's independent verifiers, JetBrains' Living Specs) pulls HN and high-quality long posts. It spreads more slowly, with better discussion.
  4. A visible pain point (pages that look AI-made) suits list posts and charts in clusters, but few people report using any single project.
