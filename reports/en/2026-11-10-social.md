- **What tends to make a feature spread** (judgment):
  - A one-command, easy-to-picture demo such as "clone any software," "rebuild Spotify" or "break a video down shot by shot." The video titles write themselves and view counts run highest.
  - Big-company or official branding plus agent-ready packaging (MCP, skill format), as with Cloudflare, Vercel, Meta, Google and DeepSeek. These climb the list fast, but organic discussion doesn't always follow.
  - "Free, no key" pitches spread quickly, but quota and privacy warnings travel with them.
