## 4. Observations

- **The harness plugin standard is converging.** The `dsh` / `dsh-plugin` tags appear on `rea`, `archify`, `ruflo`, `answer-me-with-html` and others. Together with `deepseek-harness` itself and derivatives like `ds-harness-remote`, an ecosystem is forming around a single host platform.
- **Local-first retrieval and memory are the main thread in context.** `xerj`, `graphify` and `codegraph` take the local indexing route, while `ai-memory`, `agent-memory` and `claude-mem` handle cross-session memory. What they share: they work across agents (Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, etc.) and plug in via MCP or Skill.
- **Demand for agent management and isolation is rising.** `OpenShell` (secure runtime), `orca` (runs several coding agents in parallel), `magpie` (unified model switching), and the new repos `pandacode` and `reason-network` all address "running multiple agents at once, with control and collaboration."
- **Among vertical agents, reverse engineering and security stand out.** `rea` is growing far faster than anything else, and `pentagi`, `ARTEX` and `cloudflare/security-audit-skill` are also on the list.
